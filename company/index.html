05 / Why Quartiere

Four quarters, one city.

The word comes from the Latin quartus: a fourth. In 1343 Florence reorganised itself into four quartieri: Santo Spirito, Santa Croce, Santa Maria Novella and San Giovanni. Each had its own colours, its own banners and its own citizens’ militia, and each sent its own people to govern the city. Within a century, the same four quarters produced the Renaissance, and gave a name to the idea of putting people at the centre: humanism.

That is how we see an organisation. Every team has its own way of working, and the whole only works when those teams connect. We help them connect, and we keep what makes each of them good at what they do.

A city is full of people, and it still runs on technology: water, power, roads, trade. The best of it goes unnoticed. It simply lets people get on with their lives. That is the kind of technology we want to build.

Four quarters is also where our name meets our method: the four steps of the Q4 Method, and the four marks in our logo.
