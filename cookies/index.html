Cookies.

This site does not track you.

Last updated · 23 September 2026

Short version: this website does not place cookies on visitors' devices.

01 / Visitors

Reading the site

When you browse Quartiere, we do not set any cookies and do not use similar technologies such as tracking pixels or local storage. There are no analytics, advertising or social media trackers on these pages, which is why you do not see a cookie banner.

02 / Our team

Signing in to publish

Members of our team who sign in to publish articles use one strictly necessary cookie, set only on the sign-in step:

NamePurposeDuration
decap_oauth_stateProtects the GitHub sign-in against forged requests10 minutes

The publishing editor also keeps the signed-in session in the team member's browser storage. None of this applies to visitors.

03 / Changes

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

Questions: legal@elocollective.com. See also our privacy policy.
