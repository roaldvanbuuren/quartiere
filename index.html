Human × Machine
The situation

You’ve bought the licences. The work hasn’t changed.

Most companies now pay for AI in some form. Pilots get demoed, then quietly stall. Half the team uses chatbots on their own phones, outside anything the business can see or build on.

The technology works. It just never became part of how the business runs. And the people who are meant to use it hear one word: replacement. That fear is reasonable, and it is the main reason adoption stalls.

The real question

Where does AI belong in your business, and how do you get your people to use it?

Don’t hand it to the intern.

When social media arrived, most leadership teams knew it mattered and had no idea what to do with it. So they gave it to whoever had a Twitter account, and it stayed a side project for years.

The companies that pulled ahead made it part of how they sold, served customers and hired.

AI is the same moment with far more at stake. It has to become part of how the work gets done, by the people who do it.

The Q4 Method

Four steps from first conversation to working system.

Every engagement follows the Q4 Method: see the work clearly, redesign it, put AI into it, and make sure it lasts.

01 · 3 to 4 weeks, fixed fee

Understand

We sit with your teams and map how the work actually happens: who decides what, where information gets stuck, where the hours go. You get a prioritised plan with costs and expected impact.

02

Design

We redesign the workflow around your people and choose the right tool for each step: something you already own, one of our own systems, or something built for you.

03 · 8 to 12 weeks

Build

We put the first system live inside a real workflow, built alongside the people who will use it every day.

04 · Ongoing

Enable

We help your people grow into their new work, train them to build their own tools, and keep the systems improving as your business changes.

The person stays at the centre. The system around them gets smarter.
