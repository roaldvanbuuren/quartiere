WHERE WE FOCUS

Different sectors.

Machines can search, compare, remember and handle repetition at a scale no individual can. People bring intent, practical knowledge, relationships and responsibility. We connect those strengths in the flow of real work, in four sectors where we know the work well.

01
SOFTWARE FOR INDUSTRY

Industrial software

Companies that build software for factories sit on decades of expertise: in their code, their support history and the heads of their application engineers. Most know AI matters to their product and their customers. Few have a coherent plan for it.

We work on both fronts. Inside the company: support, documentation, quoting and onboarding. Inside the product: where AI can make your software smarter for every customer who uses it.

02
PRECISION & PRODUCTION

Precision manufacturing

Experienced operators know the small details that decide setup time, material use and finish, and that knowledge rarely makes it into a system. AI can match a new job against past jobs, surface likely setup issues and make specifications quick to find.

Newer operators get up to speed faster, and experienced ones answer the same question less often. Adjustment, inspection and sign-off stay with the people who know the process.

EQUIPMENT & EXPERTISE

Machinery

Machinery companies keep their most valuable knowledge in manuals, service histories and the memory of their best technicians. AI can help a field engineer find the right fix for a specific model, link a symptom to past cases, and turn every repeat repair into shared knowledge.

The engineer still diagnoses, adapts and makes the repair, with far less searching.
