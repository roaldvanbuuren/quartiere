What we collect, why, and your rights.

Last updated · 23 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data Quartiere collects through this website, why, and what rights you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Data controller

Quartiere is a trading name of ELO Group Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16304986), registered office 173 Blythe Road, London W14 0HL, United Kingdom. It is the controller of your personal data under the UK GDPR and, for visitors in the European Union, the EU GDPR.

For anything related to your personal data, email legal@elocollective.com.

03 / Who processes it

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf, under data processing agreements:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts this website. Vercel is based in the United States; transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, and/or Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum.
  • Google Workspace (Google) hosts our email, including enquiries sent to hello@elocollective.com. Where Google processes data outside the UK and EU, transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Fonts, images and videos on this site are served from our own hosting. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts or other third parties when you read these pages.

04 / Your rights

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Email legal@elocollective.com and we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). If you live or work in the European Union, you can complain to the authority in your country, for example the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy (garanteprivacy.it).

05 / Changes

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
