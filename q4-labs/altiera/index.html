Altiera

Our own model, running where your data lives.

Altiera is our own language model, built on a proven open-source foundation and trained further on our own datasets. It runs on your servers or in a European cloud, and your data never trains a model for anyone else.

Private language model
Book a demo →How it works ↓
01 / The problem

Your most valuable data is the data you can’t send anywhere.

Drawings, process parameters, customer contracts, programme documents. For many European businesses, sending this to a public AI service is not an option, for legal, contractual or competitive reasons.

So the most valuable use cases never get started. Altiera brings the model to the data instead.

AltieraProduct screenshot · coming soon
02 / How it works

Four steps.

01

Choose where it runs

On your own servers, in your private cloud, or in a European data centre.

02

Connect

Plug it into Vigil, Polysoma or your own tools through a standard interface.

03

Point it at your terminology and documents, inside your environment.

04

Control it

In practice.

Industrial software ↗

Precision manufacturing ↗

Machinery ↗

04 / Deployment & data

The details.

Foundation
Built on a proven open-source foundation model and trained further on Quartiere’s own datasets. Details of the base model are available on request.
Hosting
On-premise, private cloud or European data centres.
Your data
Never used to train a model for anyone else. Where a system learns from your data, the model it builds is used only for you.
Languages
Multilingual, including English and Italian.
Interface
A standard API, so our systems and your existing tools can use it.
