Q4 Labs

Our own systems, used where they help.

We build proprietary systems for recurring kinds of work and use them when they are the fastest route to a result, alongside whatever your business already runs. Each one is in daily use in our own operations first.

The systems

Four systems, each built for a specific kind of work.

Together they extend what people can see, understand, decide and do. None of them is required: we use them when they fit the work better than the alternatives.

01 / Vigil Company brain

Give the organisation a memory.

Connect documents, tickets, conversations and systems, and ask from Slack, Teams or email. Anyone gets a sourced answer from what the company already knows.

Why it mattersBuilt for the moment an expert is unavailable and the answer is buried in ten years of files.

Test the decision before you make it.

Put a decision in front of a population built from what people actually did, and get a distribution of outcomes with a stated confidence level.

Why it mattersBuilt for pricing, crisis response and retention decisions that are expensive to get wrong. Follow-up questions take hours, because the population persists.
