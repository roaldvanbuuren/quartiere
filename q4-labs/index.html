01 / Q4 Labs

Machines for human work.

We build proprietary systems for specific kinds of work, then apply them as part of the intervention. They are not the destination. They are the machinery that extends what people can do.

02 / The systems

Four systems. Four kinds of intelligence.

Each system is built around a specific kind of work. Together they extend what people can see, understand, decide and do.

01 / LUMEN B2C / Audience intelligence

Understand your audience.

Turn fragmented audience behaviour into a living picture of what people want, feel and do.

What it does Lumen brings fragmented audience behaviour together so it can be understood as a living picture rather than isolated signals.
02 / POLYSOMA Decision intelligence

Simulate the decision.

Explore complex business decisions, test scenarios and understand how systems may respond before acting.

What it does Polysoma is used to model choices and test scenarios before committing resources in the real world, making complex decisions more legible.
03 / ALTIERA Organisational intelligence

Give the organisation a memory.

Connect knowledge, context and information so people can find, understand and use what the organisation already knows.

What it does Altiera connects organisational knowledge, context and information so the people doing the work can use what the organisation already knows.
04 / MERCURIA Commercial intelligence

Make commercial intelligence usable.

Bring customer, market, operational and commercial information together so teams can understand what is happening and act.

What it does Mercuria brings customer, market, operational and commercial information together, giving teams a more usable view of what is happening and where to act.
