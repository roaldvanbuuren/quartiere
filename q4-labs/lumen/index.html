Lumen

Understand your audience.

For businesses that serve consumers, Lumen turns scattered community and audience behaviour into a living picture of what people want, feel and do.

Audience intelligence · Consumer businesses
Book a demo →How it works ↓
The problem

Your audience is talking, mostly where you are not listening.

Communities form on Discord, social platforms and forums. The signals that matter most (what people love, what frustrates them, who is about to leave) are spread across thousands of conversations nobody has time to read.

Lumen reads them all and turns them into signals your teams can act on.

How it works

Four steps.

01

Connect

Lumen connects to the community spaces where your audience gathers, starting with Discord.

02

Read

It reads conversations and behaviour at scale, across channels and members.

03

Surface

Recurring friction, emerging issues and rising topics become clear signals.

04

Act

Community, product and marketing teams share one living picture instead of anecdotes.

Where it’s used

In practice.

Spot friction after a launch, see which features people talk about, and catch churn signals early.

Consumer brands ↗

Understand how fans talk about products, launches and campaigns in their own spaces.
