Vigil

Give the organisation a memory.

Vigil connects documents, tickets, conversations and systems, and lives in the tool your team already uses: Slack, Microsoft Teams or email. Anyone can ask a question there and get an answer grounded in what the company already knows, with the source attached.

Company brain
Book a demo →How it works ↓
The problem

The answer exists. Nobody can find it.

Every company has a few people everyone goes to with questions. When they are busy, on holiday or gone, work slows down or gets repeated.

The knowledge is there, spread across drives, wikis, tickets and inboxes, in several languages and years of versions. Vigil makes it usable.

How it works

Four steps.

01

Connect

Vigil links to the places your knowledge already lives, with the same access rights people have today.

02

Organise

It reads documents, tickets and conversations and turns them into one searchable picture of what the company knows.

03

Answer

People ask in Slack, Teams or email, in plain language, and get an answer with its sources attached, so they can check it.

04

Learn

Corrections and new answers flow back in, so the next person gets a better answer.

Where it’s used

In practice.

Industrial software ↗

Support engineers find past fixes and customer-specific configurations in seconds, and new hires get up to speed without shadowing for months.

Precision manufacturing ↗

Operators and planners find the right specification, setup note or past job without asking around.

Machinery ↗

Field engineers get model-specific guidance and service history on site, and every repeat fix becomes shared knowledge.

Consumer technology ↗

Product and community teams find past decisions and their context as the company grows.

Deployment & data

The details.

Where it lives
Inside the tool your team already uses every day: Slack, Microsoft Teams or email. One place to ask, whichever system the answer is stored in. No new app to open.
Typical sources
Shared drives, wikis, ticketing and support systems, CRM, product documentation and email archives.
Access
Respects the permissions you already have: people only get answers from sources they can already open.
Answers
Every answer shows where it came from, so people can verify it and trust it.
Hosting
Runs in a European cloud or on your own servers, and can run on Altiera so no data leaves your environment.
