01 / Understand

See the work clearly before you change it.

Most AI programmes start with a tool. We start with the work. In three to four weeks we map how your business actually runs, find where time and judgement are lost, and hand you a costed plan that says where AI will pay back first, and where it will not.

What you get

  • A map of how the work really flows. People, decisions, information and systems, including the parts nobody writes down.
  • Where the hours and the judgement go. The specific handoffs, searches, retyping and waiting that slow good people down, sized in time and cost.
  • A costed plan ranked by impact. Each opportunity with expected effort, cost, payback and risk, and a clear first move.
  • A tool choice for each step. Something you already own, one of our systems, or something built. Vendor-neutral, with the reasoning written down.
  • A readout to your leadership team, plus a shorter version for the people whose work is in scope.

What we need from you

  • One sponsor with authority over the workflows in scope.
  • Access to 8 to 12 people who do the work, about 2 hours each.
  • Read access to the relevant tools, documents and process data, under NDA.

How we work

Senior people, hands on.

The people who scope the work are the people who do it. No junior teams learning on your time.

People in from day one.

We work with the teams doing the job, so the plan reflects reality and has their support before anything is built.

A decision, not a deck.

You finish with a clear first move and its business case. If the answer is "not yet", we will tell you.

How the four weeks run

Week 1

Frame

Kick-off with leadership. Agree the workflows in scope and what success means.

Week 2

Observe

Time with the teams doing the work. Interviews, shadowing, and a look at the tools and data.

Week 3

Size

Quantify the losses, test the opportunities with the people involved, cost the options.

Week 3–4

Decide

Plan written, reviewed with you, presented to leadership.

What happens next

Understand stands on its own. The plan is yours whether or not you continue with us. If you do, it feeds straight into the next steps of the Q4 Method:

  • Design. Redesign the chosen workflow and decide where AI takes the load and where people keep the judgement.
  • Build, 8 to 12 weeks. The first system live in daily work, built alongside the people who use it.
  • Enable, ongoing. Training, internal champions and a system that keeps improving.
