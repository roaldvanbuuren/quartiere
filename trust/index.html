Trust

Trust.

Your data stays where you decide, and it never trains a model for anyone else.

Much of the knowledge that makes a European business valuable cannot be sent to a public AI service. We design every system around that fact, from where it runs to who can see what.

Principles

How we handle data.

Where it runs
On your own servers, in your private cloud, or in European data centres. For the most sensitive work, Altiera runs entirely inside your environment.
Your data
Client data is never used to train a model for anyone else. Where a system learns from your data, such as a Polysoma population, the model it builds belongs to your engagement and is used only for you.
Access
Our systems respect the permissions you already have. People only get answers from sources they are allowed to open.
Human oversight
The systems we build support decisions; they do not make them. Where judgement matters, a person reviews, approves and stays accountable.
Transparency
Answers show their sources. Simulations show their assumptions. If a system cannot explain itself, we do not ship it into a decision.
Tool choice
We are vendor-neutral. When we recommend a third-party tool, we explain what data it touches and where that data goes.
Regulation

Built with the EU AI Act in mind.

Europe now regulates AI by risk. Part of the Understand step is working out which of your use cases fall under which obligations, and designing and documenting each system accordingly, so compliance is part of the build rather than a review at the end.
