Human × Machine.

Your business on one side. The technology on the other. We connect them.

03 / The Q4 Method

Four steps. One working system at the end.

Every engagement follows the same four steps. Each one ends with something concrete in your hands, so you can decide at every stage whether to go further.

01 / UNDERSTAND · 3 TO 4 WEEKS · FIXED FEE

See the system.

Map how work actually happens: people, decisions, information, constraints and habits. We sit with the teams doing the work, not only the people managing it.

You getA clear picture of where time and judgement are lost, and a costed plan ranked by impact.

02 / DESIGN

Redesign the work.

Rework workflows around how your people operate, and decide where AI takes the load and where people keep the judgement.

You getA redesigned workflow, a tool choice for each step, and the reasoning behind it.

03 / BUILD · 8 TO 12 WEEKS

Put intelligence into it.

Integrate, configure or build the system, and put it live in daily work alongside the people who will use it every day.

You getA working system used by a real team, measured against the plan.

04 / ENABLE · ONGOING

Make it last.

Train teams, develop internal champions, and keep improving the system as it learns from use and as your business changes.

You getPeople who use the tools with confidence, and a business that can keep building.

04 / Build your own

The best result is a team that can build for itself.

The people closest to the work know exactly where the friction is. With the right tools and some training, many of them can build their own AI assistants and automations.

We set up the guardrails, train a group of internal champions, and support them as they go.

05 / The principle

The machine should extend the person, not ask the person to become the machine.
