The Q4 Method

Four steps. One working system at the end.

Every engagement follows the same four steps. Each one ends with something concrete in your hands, so you can decide at every stage whether to go further.

01 / Understand · 3 to 4 weeks · Fixed fee

See the system.

Map how work actually happens: people, decisions, information, constraints and habits. We sit with leadership and with the teams doing the work, and ask each how the work feels to them.

You getA clear picture of where time and judgement are lost, where the organisation itself slows the work down, and a costed plan ranked by impact.

02 / Design

Redesign the work.

Rework workflows around how your people operate, decide where AI takes the load and where people keep the judgement, and redesign the roles that change as a result.

You getA redesigned workflow, a tool choice for each step, and a clear picture of how each affected role will change.

03 / Build · 8 to 12 weeks

Put intelligence into it.

Integrate, configure or build the system, and put it live in daily work alongside the people who will use it every day.

You getA working system used by a real team, measured against the plan.

04 / Enable · Ongoing

Make it last.

When AI takes on part of a role, we redefine that role with the person who holds it, so everyone knows what they own and where they can grow. Then we train teams, develop internal champions and keep improving the system as your business changes.

You getPeople who have grown into better work, use the tools with confidence, and can keep building.

Build your own

The best result is a team that can build for itself.

The people closest to the work know exactly where the friction is. With the right tools and some training, many of them can build their own AI assistants and automations.

We set up the guardrails, train a group of internal champions, and support them as they go.

The principle

The machine should extend the person, not ask the person to become the machine.
